MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (MSA)
Contract ID: MSA-2026-NX89
Effective Date: March 15, 2026
Provider: Nexus Enterprise Solutions LLC
Client: Apex Logistics Ltd.

1. SCOPE OF SERVICES
Nexus Enterprise Solutions agrees to provide Apex Logistics with cloud infrastructure optimization, 24/7 database monitoring, and custom microservice development. Any additional out-of-scope tasks require a formal Change Order approved by both parties.

2. FINANCIAL TERMS & PAYMENT SCHEDULING
- Base Retainer: Apex Logistics shall pay a monthly retainer fee of $18,500 USD.
- Payment Schedule: Invoices will be generated on the 1st business day of each calendar month and must be settled within net-30 days via wire transfer.
- Late Payment Penalty: Late payments beyond 30 days will accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month on the outstanding balance.

3. SLA & SYSTEM AVAILABILITY
Nexus guarantees a 99.95% operational uptime for core database routing layers. If monthly uptime drops below 99.95%, Apex Logistics is entitled to a service credit equivalent to 5% of the monthly retainer for every 0.1% of downtime, capped at a maximum 25% credit per billing cycle.

4. TERMINATION & RENEWAL
This agreement runs for 12 months, expiring on March 14, 2027. Either party may terminate this agreement without cause by providing a written 60-day notice. Immediate termination is permitted in the event of a material breach uncured within 14 days of written notice.
